# Hardware Components

The physical inverted pendulum prototype was built using the following components:

- Arduino UNO
- 2 DC gear motors
- 2 motor-driven wheels
- 1 front caster wheel
- MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope
- L298N motor driver
- 12 V battery supply
- Pendulum rod
- Pivot and bearing mechanism
- Base/chassis
- Breadboard and jumper wires
